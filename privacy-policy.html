Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This privacy policy explains what personal data oyenino.com (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our website and free resources, and how that data is used.

1. Data we collect

When you submit a form on our site (for example, to unlock a free guide, prompt or tool), we collect:

  • Information you provide: full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Marketing preference: whether you opted in to receive marketing emails (recorded separately from your access request).
  • Device information (collected automatically): browser type, operating system, device type (mobile/tablet/desktop), screen size, viewport size, language, timezone, and connection type.
  • Approximate location (collected automatically via your IP address): city, region, country, coordinates, ISP, and postal code. This is obtained through the third-party service ipapi.co.
  • Bot protection token: a Cloudflare Turnstile verification token confirming you are a human visitor.

2. Why we collect this data

  • Name, email and phone: to deliver the free resource you requested and to follow up about your request if needed.
  • Device and location data: to understand our audience, prevent abuse, and improve our resources.
  • Turnstile token: to protect our forms from automated spam and abuse.
  • Marketing preference: to send learning tips and new resource announcements only if you have explicitly opted in.

3. How we store your data

Form submissions are sent to our backend service at forms.oyenino.com. Your data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Data retention: We have not yet published a specific data retention period. If you would like your data deleted, please contact us at the address below and we will process your request. We intend to formalize a retention policy — this section will be updated when that is in place.

4. Third-party services

Our site uses the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID: G-BGL94NLVL7): collects anonymized usage data such as page views, scroll depth and button clicks. We do not send your name, email or phone number to Google Analytics.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot verification widget. Cloudflare processes a challenge token to confirm you are human. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • ipapi.co: used to determine your approximate location from your IP address. See ipapi.co's privacy policy.
  • Google DNS (dns.google): used to verify your email domain has valid MX records. Only the email domain (not your full email address) is sent.
  • Google Fonts: loads typefaces from Google's servers. See Google's privacy policy.

5. Marketing communications

We only send marketing emails if you explicitly check the opt-in box on our forms. Submitting a form to access a free resource does not subscribe you to marketing emails. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

6. Cookies and local storage

Our site uses sessionStorage (cleared when you close the browser tab) to remember that you have already submitted a form during your current session. This avoids asking you to fill out the form again on the same page. We do not use persistent cookies for tracking. Google Analytics and Cloudflare Turnstile may set their own cookies as described in their respective privacy policies.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time.
  • Object to data processing where applicable.
Note: We are a small independent site and do not yet have a formal data subject access request process. For any privacy-related requests, please contact us directly. We will respond as quickly as possible.

8. Children's privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Himanshu via the contact form on the homepage or reach out on Instagram (@oui.nino).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
