Questions people ask

What is a home server?

A home server is a small computer that stays on at home and does jobs for you, such as backing up your phone photos, blocking ads on your Wi-Fi or playing your own videos on your TV. It can be an old laptop, a mini PC or a Raspberry Pi.

Do I need a home server?

Maybe not. A home server makes sense if your cloud storage is always full, you want control over where your photos and files are kept, or you enjoy learning how things work. It needs regular updates and backups, so it is not a good fit if you want something that never needs your attention. The Beginner guide helps you decide before you buy anything.

What hardware do I need to start?

You can start with a computer you already own, such as an old laptop. The Tech guide uses a Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 GB of RAM, its official power supply and an SSD in a USB 3 adapter. A network cable and a second drive for backups are also useful.

Do I need to know coding or Linux?

No. The Beginner guide uses plain words, and the AI prompts help an AI chat such as ChatGPT or Claude walk you through one step at a time. The Tech guide is written for people who are comfortable with a command line.
